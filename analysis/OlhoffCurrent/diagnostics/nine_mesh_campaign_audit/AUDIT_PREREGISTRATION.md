# Audit preregistration

Recorded 2026-09-12, before reading final three-rung campaign outcomes. This is a retrospective audit protocol, not a prospective registration of the campaign.

The intended series is 160x20 through 800x100 in steps of 80x10. The intended physical filter is R/b=0.06, the move ladder [0.04,0.02,0.01], and the frozen stageExhaustion detector is E=A OR B with persistent terminal E. These are hypotheses to verify from stored effective configurations and source, not assumptions of success.

Absolute lock: no optimizer execution, reruns, repairs, retuning, cap extension, or production edits. All new files remain in this directory. Only stored evidence, source inspection, offline detector replay, statistics, plotting and paper inspection are permitted.

Falsification order: establish campaign identity and retained-evidence integrity; distinguish valid capped observations from convergence; independently reconstruct controller events and endpoint maturity; test full-sequence objective, topology and spectral consistency; then assess causal common-mesh savings and practical scaling. Missing counterfactuals remain missing. A fired Boolean is not proof of stationarity, optimality or a continuum limit.

Metrics: endpoint and trailing 20/50/100-step changes; stagewise detector margins and inner work; physical-coordinate adjacent-density differences and thresholded overlap; descriptive log-log fits with prefactor, exponent, residuals and sensitivity to fine-only subsets. Do not force an asymptotic refinement fit on an oscillatory or bifurcating sequence. Treat timing as observational single-run data, with confidence intervals conditional on regression assumptions.

Integrity labels: INVALID for demonstrated corruption/formulation mismatch preventing intended interpretation; INCOMPLETE for missing terminal/required trajectory evidence; VALID_WITH_CAVEAT for usable evidence with explicit provenance/telemetry limitations; VALID when required evidence and formulation checks pass. CAP_HIT is a valid unsuccessful observation if evidence is complete. Declare overall campaign, controller, termination, objective, topology, multiplicity, performance and value separately, and choose exactly one programme recommendation.
