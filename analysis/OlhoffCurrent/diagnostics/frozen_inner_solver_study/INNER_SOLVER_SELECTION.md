# Selected future candidate

C. Exact SOCP, restricted to the proven N=2 reconstruction and independently certified before any future outer update.

Exact SOCP is the only measured realization satisfying every preregistered objective, design, active-bound, feasibility and exact-KKT bar. Both fresh repetitions reproduce the reference bit-for-bit and obtain an independently checked dual gap of about 9.15e-12. Solve cost is about 45.5 seconds, with assembly and certification recorded separately. The certificate is essential because coneprog's returned duals and exitflag are insufficient. The best approximate endpoint, S34 at 500 calls, recovers 99.9349% of gain but has d2=0.199492, dinf=1.102358, bound agreement=22.5889%, and KKT=3.52387e-4; it is not a certified correction. Selection rests on mathematical and numerical fidelity, not speed. Only the fixed N=2 scope is proposed; reliability throughout a future topology trajectory remains to be tested.

The selection is for a future experiment only. No production adapter was
installed. No drho was applied. A method can be mathematically legitimate and
still fail the stringent finite-work fidelity test; GCMMA is not called globally
convergent on the strength of one frozen state.
