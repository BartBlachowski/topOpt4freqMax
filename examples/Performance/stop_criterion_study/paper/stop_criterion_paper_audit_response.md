# Response to the audit of `stop_criterion_paper.tex`

Revised 13 October 2026. Every audit item was checked against `scripts/replay.py`, `scripts/stopstudy_metrics.m`, the recorded CSV histories and the frozen profiles before being accepted or declined. The manuscript was rewritten; all numbers in the tables are unchanged.

## 1. Interpretation items

| Item | Decision | What changed |

"No universal tolerance exists" and "norms of any kind" removed; R1 described as calibrated and assessed on these records; Section 5.1 retitled "Limitations of the tested design-change criteria". |

## 2. Factual and numerical corrections

All accepted and applied: three approaches (not two); Yuksel–Yilmaz move limit 0.1 (profile `yuksel_practical_move01_tol001`; the 0.2 label in the original figure title was also wrong and the figures were regenerated into `paper/fig/` with corrected labels); quasi-static load described as built from a fixed reference mode and the current mass matrix; nine meshes per static method and five for Du–Olhoff; Du–Olhoff ranges 0.011–0.073 and 1.5–6.4·10⁻³; "a change of 0.001 in the threshold"; R1 removed from the list of criteria producing long tails on the coarse Du–Olhoff meshes and the RMS 3.5·10⁻³ / relative-L₂ 5·10⁻³ thresholds distinguished; Yuksel figure caption now says max-change "repeatedly reaches" the move limit; objective-only example is 800×100 at 1.09 %; grayness-only 2.71 %, flip 2.82 / 3.29 %, max-change 0.025 "failed to terminate three records"; M_nd 10⁻² variant has the same worst deficit but a different median ratio (1.25 vs 1.13); W = 20 overhead stated via the median ratio and the tripling on the coarsest quasi-static mesh; 93–121; quasi-static only for the late-tail M_nd decrease, with the Yuksel–Yilmaz increase stated; "different native stopping criteria"; Table A1 headers give the equivalent Euclidean-norm inequalities; Table A3/A4 headings use range(f) and range(M_nd).

## 3. Language and presentation

Applied: all suggested replacements in the audit's table; "stopping criterion" used throughout (one definition of "native" criterion added at the start of Section 2); "recorded run / record / history" used with the distinction record = file, history = its content; American spelling; the Introduction's literature paragraph shortened to one paragraph that points to Section 2; the long paragraph of Section 5.1 split into premature stopping, the two-phase 800×100 history, and the comparison of criteria; the signed deficit defined as Eq. Adopted as suggested, with the 0.91 % result retained because the tables were not regenerated (see 1.4).
