## Interpretation rule

Historical phrases such as “exact,” “faithful,” “identical,” “reference,”
“benchmark,” “migrate,” “production,” or “freeze” record an intermediate
assessment and are superseded. None of the archived outputs may be used to:

- claim reproduction of Du and Olhoff (2007);
- estimate a gap to the published optimum;
- rank convergence against the proposed method;
- calculate a speedup or scaling exponent;
- supply a reviewer-facing topology, result table, or convergence figure.

The archive supports only the negative scientific conclusion: the attempted
reconstruction did not establish paper fidelity after extensive diagnostics,
leaving benchmark under-specification or unpublished implementation details as
the most plausible explanation.

Active revision comparisons use `analysis/OlhoffApproach` only and identify it
as a local Olhoff-inspired implementation.
