                                                              Previous work has mainly focused on this problem as a re-
                                                              inforcement problem using the homogenization method.
                                                              This has the advantage that it removes the possibility of
                                                              localized modes in low density areas. It is shown here that
                                                              the basic mechanism for these local modes is the diﬀer-
                                                              ence in the interpolation of the stiﬀness and the mass.
                                                              A numerical method of removing the problem of localized
                                                              modes is proposed. It consists of two aspects: the SIMP
                                                              method is changed for density factors lower the 10% and
                                                              the nodes in low density areas are neglected in the con-
                                                              vergence criterion of the eigenvalue problem. The SIMP
                                                              method is changed by restricting the maximum value of
                                                              the ratio between the penalization of the stiﬀness and the
Fig. 16 Optimized design of AFM probe when there is no        penalization of the mass. This is achieved by changing
constraint in higher frequencies                              the penalization of the stiﬀness when the density factor
                                                              is lower than 10%; it is proposed here that the penaliza-
                                                              tion of the stiﬀness is one hundredth of the penalization of
                                                              the mass. By using the proposed scheme the optimization
                                                              problem does not need to be formulated as reinforcement
                                                              problem.
                                                                  Two examples found in literature are used as inspira-
                                                              tion for the two examples shown. The results show that it
                                                              is possible to solve topology optimization problems using
                                                              the SIMP method without formulating the problem as
                                                              a reinforcement problem.
